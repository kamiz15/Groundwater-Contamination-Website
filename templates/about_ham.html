A 2D model that estimates the steady-state plume length (Lmax) and explores the effect of transverse dispersivity on the length of a plume degraded by an instantaneous bimolecular reaction at its fringe. In CAST the source is characterised by its flux Q.

  • An instantaneous reaction between the injected compound and a reactant in the ambient groundwater at the plume fringe.
