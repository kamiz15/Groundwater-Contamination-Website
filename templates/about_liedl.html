A vertically oriented 2D model that estimates the steady-state length of a mixing-controlled plume (Lmax). The vertical orientation refers to the z-axis being aligned with the aquifer thickness. Degradation occurs by an instantaneous bimolecular reaction confined to the plume fringe.

{% endblock %} {% block about_assumptions_class %} about-assumptions--check{% endblock %} {% block about_assumptions %}
  • Steady-state condition in a homogeneous and isotropic aquifer.
  • A single-step bimolecular instantaneous reaction between reactants taking place only at the fringe.
  • The contaminant source (electron donor) penetrates the entire thickness of the aquifer.
