Privacy Policy

CAST is a non-commercial research platform operated as a joint project of the Eberhard Karls University of Tübingen, the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, and partner universities. Each regional deployment is independent: your account and data exist only on the server you registered on and are never transferred to the other region.

What we store

  • Account data: username, email address, and your password as a salted cryptographic hash. We never store the password itself.
  • Research data: the site/measurement datasets you upload, linked to your account and visible only to you.
  • Technical logs: web-server logs including IP addresses, kept for a short period (automatic rotation) for security and debugging.

We use no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and we do not share or sell any data. The only cookie is the strictly necessary login-session cookie.

Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed solely to provide your account and store your research datasets (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); for the Indian deployment, consent given at registration under the DPDP Act 2023).

Retention and deletion

Your account and all data linked to it are kept until you request deletion. On request we delete the account and every dataset it owns, permanently. Contact: [FILL IN: contact email].

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time. EU users may additionally lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority ([FILL IN: LfDI Baden-Württemberg contact for the German deployment]). Users of the Indian deployment may escalate grievances to the Data Protection Board of India.
