A data-driven surrogate for BIOSCREEN-AT. Two second-order polynomials were fitted to roughly 1000 synthetic BIOSCREEN-AT runs, and between them they return the maximum plume length (Lmax) and the time taken to reach it (TLmax) from three field parameters, algebraically — where BIOSCREEN-AT itself needs more than ten parameters and an iterative search.

The A and B coefficients are empirical, fixed by the publication and not physically interpretable. They are not inputs.

{% endblock %} {% block about_equation %} Lmax = A1+ A2λe+ A3v+ A4λe2+ A5v2+ A6λev TLmax = B1+ B2λe+ B3Γ+ B4λe2+ B5Γ2+ B6λeΓ {% endblock %} {% block about_equation_note %}

Eqs. (13) and (14); coefficients from Table 2 of the publication. The units are those the coefficients were fitted in and are not interchangeable — a velocity in m/day produces a number, and it is wrong.

Outside these ranges the polynomials extrapolate. The model returns the value and flags it rather than refusing to run — the publication extrapolates too, as both of its field sites sit outside the λe band.

{% endblock %} {% block about_parameters %}
λe
Effective first-order decay rate constant in the plume (1/yr)
v
Linear (average) groundwater seepage velocity (m/yr)
Γ
Source decay rate constant (1/yr). Not the stoichiometric coefficient γ of the Liedl, Cirpka, Maier and Ham models — this is the quantity BIOSCREEN calls the source decay coefficient.
{% endblock %} {% block about_assumptions %}
  • The polynomials are surrogates for BIOSCREEN-AT and inherit its assumptions: a uniform flow field, a rectangular patch source, and instantaneous reaction between donor and acceptor.
  • The fitted coefficients are fixed by the publication and are not physically interpretable, so they are never exposed as inputs.
  • Lmax is not monotonic in λe. Eq. (13) is a conic section with A4>0, so it has a minimum at λ*=5808.705+85.617v2⋅10338.393 — about 0.36 1/yr at v=20 m/yr, inside the fitted band. A slider dragged through that point visibly reverses direction. This is the published model behaving as published.
  • An extreme parameter combination can drive either output negative. The raw value is returned rather than clipped, so the condition stays visible.
