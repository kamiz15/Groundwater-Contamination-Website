Single-Source Inverse Modelling

Given ~4 observed plume-length signals (each paired with a known effective source thickness M), this tool iterates the Liedl analytical forward model to recover the transverse dispersivity αTv that best explains all observations simultaneously.

How it works: Each signal is one field observation: a source thickness M measured from a borehole or source geometry estimate, and an observed plume length from field mapping. The solver (Brent bounded minimiser) iterates the Liedl forward model to minimise the sum of squared plume-length residuals across all signals. A non-converged result is flagged and never displayed as a usable fit.

Out of scope: multi-source inversion, irregular source geometry.
